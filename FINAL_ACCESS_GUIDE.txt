========================================
   ACCESS STUDENT-INSTRUCTOR VIEW
========================================

STEP 1: Start Server
--------------------
python manage.py runserver


STEP 2: Login as Admin
-----------------------
Go to: http://127.0.0.1:8000/accounts/login/
Username: admin
Password: admin123


STEP 3: Go to Dashboard
------------------------
After login, you'll be redirected to:
http://127.0.0.1:8000/accounts/dashboard/

OR click on your username → Dashboard


STEP 4: Scroll Down
--------------------
You'll see a section called:
"Student-Instructor Management"

With 4 buttons:
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Student-Instructor Management       │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [Students by Instructor]            │ ← CLICK THIS!
│ [Instructors by Student]            │
│ [All Instructors]                   │
│ [Student Progress]                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘


STEP 5: Click "Students by Instructor"
---------------------------------------
This shows:
- Which students are learning under each instructor
- What courses they're taking
- Their progress


========================================
           THAT'S IT!
========================================

Login → Dashboard → Scroll Down → Click Button

You'll see all students grouped by their instructors!
